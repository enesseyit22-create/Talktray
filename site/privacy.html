Privacy Policy

Last updated: 10 November 2026

Talktray is a voice inbox. This policy explains what the app handles and why. It applies to the Talktray mobile apps and the pages on this site.

What we collect

  • Account. If you create an account, we store your email address, a display name, your language, timezone, and theme.
  • Voice and text. When you record, the audio, the transcript, and the tasks, reminders, events, shopping items, and notes you save are stored for your account.
  • Usage. We store a monthly recording count so the free plan and Talktray Pro fair-use cap can be applied.
  • Purchases. Subscription status is handled by Apple, Google, and RevenueCat. Talktray does not receive your full payment card number.
  • Device permissions. The microphone is used only while you are recording. Calendar and reminders access is used only after you confirm an item should be saved there. The app does not record in the background.

If you stay in demo mode, recordings and inbox items stay on that device and are not uploaded.

Why we use it

We use this information to sign you in, transcribe what you said, sort it into inbox items, show reminders, and run the subscription. Audio is sent to the transcription provider only to produce the transcript you review.

Sharing

We do not sell personal information. Processors that help run Talktray include Supabase (account and database), the speech and language provider configured for transcription, RevenueCat, and Apple or Google for payments and sign-in.

How long we keep it

We keep account data and inbox content until you delete the account in the app, or until you delete an individual item. Deleting the account removes the profile, recordings, inbox items, usage records, and stored audio tied to that user, then signs you out.

Your choices

You can change language and notification settings in Profile, deny microphone or calendar access in system settings, and delete your account from Settings. Demo data can be cleared by deleting the account or removing sample items. Steps, including how to ask for deletion without the app, are on the delete account page.
