# Talktray — English

## App Store

**Name:** Talktray: AI Voice Inbox

**Subtitle:** Voice notes & transcription

**Keywords:** voicenotes,transcription,ai,inbox,reminders,tasks,dictation,speech,memo,todo,notes

**Promotional text:** Say it once. Talktray turns a spoken thought into tasks, reminders, events, and notes, then keeps them in one voice inbox.

**Description:**

Talktray is a voice inbox for the thoughts you do not want to type. Tap the mic, speak a task, a reminder, a grocery run, or a half-formed idea, and Talktray transcribes it and sorts the pieces.

What you can do
• Capture voice notes in a tap
• Turn speech into tasks, reminders, events, shopping lists, and notes
• Review the transcript before anything is saved
• Keep local reminders on your phone
• Export confirmed events to your calendar

Talktray Pro keeps the month open, with a fair-use cap and no ads. The free inbox holds 25 recordings a month.

Built for people who think out loud: students, founders, parents, and anyone whose best ideas show up while walking, driving, or halfway through making coffee.
