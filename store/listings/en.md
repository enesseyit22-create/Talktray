# Talktray — English

## App Store

**Name:** Talktray: Voice Notes to Tasks

**Subtitle:** AI Transcription & Reminders

**Keywords:** todo,list,dictation,speech,text,memo,recorder,planner,inbox,organizer,calendar,shopping,ideas

**Promotional text:** Say it once. Talktray turns one voice note into tasks, reminders, events, shopping items and notes. Review it, save it and get on with your day.

**Description:**

Talktray is the AI voice inbox for everything you don't want to type. Tap the mic, say what's on your mind, and Talktray transcribes your voice note and turns it into tasks, reminders, calendar events, shopping items and notes, all from one recording.

Say "Call the bank tomorrow at 10, buy milk and eggs, dentist on Friday at 4" and you get a task, a shopping list and an event, each with the right date and time. Review everything before it's saved, fix anything with a tap, and move on.

HOW IT WORKS
1. Tap record and speak naturally, in your own words.
2. AI transcription turns your speech into text.
3. Talktray splits the thought into tasks, reminders, events, shopping items and notes.
4. Check the result, edit if needed, and save.

WHAT YOU GET
• Voice notes to to-do list: speak your tasks instead of typing them
• Reminders with dates and times, scheduled on your phone
• Events you can add to your calendar after you confirm them
• Shopping lists pulled straight from what you said
• Notes and ideas kept in one tidy voice inbox
• Today and overdue views so nothing slips
• Home screen widget for one-tap recording
• Ten app languages: English, Turkish, Spanish, Portuguese, German, French, Italian, Japanese, Korean and Indonesian
• Light and dark mode

BUILT FOR PEOPLE WHO THINK OUT LOUD
Students, founders, parents, commuters and anyone whose best ideas show up halfway through making coffee. Use Talktray as a voice recorder, a dictation app, a to-do list or a simple reminder app.

YOUR VOICE, YOUR CHOICE
Talktray only uses the microphone while you are recording. It does not record in the background. Nothing is saved until you confirm it.

FREE AND PRO
• Free: 25 recordings a month
• Talktray Pro: $7.99 a month or $49.99 a year, with a generous fair-use cap
• No ads on any plan
Prices are in US dollars; your local price is shown in the app before you buy. Subscriptions renew automatically unless cancelled at least 24 hours before the end of the current period. You can manage or cancel them in your account settings.

Say it once. Talktray sorts it.
